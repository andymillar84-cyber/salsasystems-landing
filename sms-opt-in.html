How SMS opt-in works

Salsa Systems, a trading name of Andrew Riascos Millar (ABN 20 588 929 132)

Pool-building business owners enquire with Salsa Systems through our Facebook and Instagram lead ads (Meta Instant Form). The form collects email, phone number, first name, last name, city and company name. Before submitting, the person sees a "Text message consent" step with an optional, unticked checkbox. Ticking it is not required to submit the form or to buy anything. Only people who tick it receive text messages from us.
